# Contribute to the PLAYTEX AI Creator Playground

Share a scene made with the samples, report a confusing guide, or request a practical workflow through Issues. Include your renderer/version, the asset or guide, and the expected result. Screenshots are useful when you own or have permission to share them.

Keep submissions focused on public documentation and examples. Do not post passwords, API keys, private project links, customer files, or product source. For account or billing help, use the support channel on https://www.playtex.ai/ rather than a public issue.

Documentation changes should explain the behavior plainly, preserve PLAYTEX AI branding, and distinguish AI source imagery from deterministic PBR processing. Claims of native-engine compatibility need a concrete tested version and result. New assets require an explicit source, creator, redistribution license, and attribution record before inclusion.
