# PLAYTEX AI — Wood Photo PBR

A real first-party wood-photo crop with seven 512 × 512 PNG maps from the PLAYTEX AI PBR reference core. These are the exact outputs already published in our September 3, 2026 image-to-PBR example.

Includes source, albedo, OpenGL (+Y) normal, roughness, metallic, height, ambient occlusion, emission, and processing settings. Treat albedo/emission as color; other maps as linear data. The source is not seamless or a measured scan. Start with emission disabled and metallic at zero for ordinary wood; the exported channels are processing examples, not measured material properties. Review scale and lighting in your renderer.
